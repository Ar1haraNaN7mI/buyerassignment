## Part One: Generative AI

### Core Issues With the Website

Generative AI was first used as a diagnostic tool to identify likely issues in the Beyond Bouldering website from the perspective of a first-time visitor. The AI analysis suggested that the original experience could be improved in four areas: unclear beginner onboarding, fragmented pricing comparison, weak connection between online browsing and waiver completion, and limited routine-based location guidance. These issues are meaningful because a new user does not only evaluate the visual appeal of a climbing gym; they also need to understand safety, equipment, price commitment, location convenience, and pre-arrival requirements before deciding to visit.

The four core issues from Assignment 2 can be summarised as follows. First, the website needs a clearer path for beginners who may not understand bouldering rules, equipment hire, or arrival expectations. Second, pricing should be easier to compare because casual entry, starter packs, visit passes, and memberships represent different levels of commitment. Third, local search and location choice should be stronger because Adelaide users may search by suburb, CBD proximity, or commuting routine. Fourth, the website should better connect the digital journey with the physical visit, especially through waiver and check-in guidance.

The AI-identified issues and the Assignment 2 issues overlap strongly, but they are not identical. The AI emphasised cognitive friction and conversion flow, while the Assignment 2 issues also focused on digital marketing outcomes such as SEO and strategic content. The critical evaluation therefore refined the problem from "the website needs more information" to "the website needs better sequencing of decision-support information." Adding more content alone would not solve the problem if users still had to search across the page for beginner guidance, pricing logic, and location relevance.

The revised core issues used for the prototype are: a lack of explicit first-time visitor onboarding; insufficient side-by-side pricing decision support; weak integration of waiver preparation into the conversion path; and underdeveloped local SEO/location messaging. These revised issues directly informed the website structure, including the First Time section, pricing cards, location tabs, SEO keyword block, and waiver form.

### Keywords

Generative AI was also used to generate an initial keyword list for Beyond Bouldering. The AI suggested phrases such as "bouldering for beginners in Adelaide," "indoor bouldering Adelaide CBD," "beginner climbing gym Kent Town," "climbing gym Keswick," "bouldering classes Adelaide," and "social fitness Adelaide." These terms are useful because they reflect intent rather than only brand awareness. A user who has not yet chosen Beyond Bouldering may search for a beginner activity, a nearby climbing gym, or a class-based fitness option.

The SEMrush keyword list should be inserted here after manual research. The SEMrush terms should be compared with the AI terms by search volume, keyword difficulty, user intent, and relevance to the website content. For example, broad terms may attract more search volume but may not convert as well as suburb-specific or beginner-specific long-tail terms. Conversely, a very specific term may be easier to rank for but may have limited traffic potential.

The critical keyword evaluation should focus on whether each term supports the redesigned customer journey. Keywords connected to beginner guidance should appear in the First Time section. Keywords connected to Adelaide, CBD, Kent Town, Keswick, and Thebarton should support the Locations section. Keywords connected to classes or coaching should appear in the Classes and Community section. The final statement of keywords should prioritise long-tail local intent, because this better matches the behaviour of users who are actively planning a visit rather than casually browsing.

**Final keyword statement:** The redesigned website should prioritise beginner, local, and class-related search intent. The selected keyword set should include a mix of AI-suggested long-tail phrases and SEMrush-validated search terms, with final placement aligned to the user journey rather than inserted as isolated keyword blocks.
